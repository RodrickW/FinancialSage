Hey,
Spent time going through every screen of the app. Here's my honest take.
What's working:

Brand identity is solid the green palette, logo, and name all feel
cohesive and approachable
Faith Mode is a genuine differentiator. No major competitor (Mint, YNAB,
Monarch) has this. You're sitting on a blue ocean and barely using it
Pricing is well calibrated $5.99/$9.99 is accessible and the free tier
builds trust
The Goals page has real personality the badges, levels, and streaks show
good instincts around gamification. Bring that energy to the rest of the
app
The AI Coach concept is smart. The suggested prompt chips are a nice
touch

What needs fixing:
The home page leads with a paywall before delivering any value. The
first thing a new user sees is a locked upgrade card before they've done
anything. Show value first, ask for money second. Also, having two
separate upgrade CTAs on the same screen creates ad-blindness. Pick one
and remove the other.
The Budget page is a plain text list with $0/$0 next to everything. This
is your core feature and it has no visual expression whatsoever. Even
simple progress bars per category would be a meaningful improvement. A
donut/wheel chart would be transformative.
The Playbook page is just an empty white box for new users. It's your
most interesting concept a personalized 30-day plan based on money
personality — and it's completely invisible until someone subscribes.
Show a blurred preview so users can see what they're working toward.
Settings has two things: read-only profile info and a delete account
button. No password change, no notifications, no profile photo. For a
financial app this is a trust issue. Users need to feel in control of
their data.
The AI Coach only speaks when spoken to. A real coach watches your back
without being asked. Even a proactive opening message when users log in
("Here's something I noticed about your finances this week...") would
change the feel entirely.
There's no data visualization anywhere in the app. No charts, no graphs,
no trend lines. In 2026 a finance app without visuals feels unfinished.
Biggest opportunities:

Make the Money Mind Interview the first thing every new user does before
connecting an account. The personality type reveal creates an immediate
"this app gets me" moment and gives users a reason to come back
Go all-in on Faith Mode. Make it a full experience — scripture alongside
decisions, "Stewardship" replacing "Budget" in the nav, a tithing
calculator, a Generosity Score. Then market it directly to churches and
Christian finance influencers. Nobody owns this space
Add a "What If" simulator — sliders that show the compound impact of
small changes ("What if I put $50/month toward debt?"). This is the
feature people screenshot and share
Build a Debt Payoff visualizer with snowball/avalanche toggles and a
debt-free date counter. People share their debt-free dates. It's viral
by design
End-of-month "Money Story" recap a Spotify Wrapped for your finances.
Shareable, visual, personal. This is a referral loop built into the
product

Ship these this week:

Replace the empty $0 cards with a setup checklist (Connect account → Set
budget → Create goal → Complete Playbook)
Add progress bars to every budget category row
Show a blurred Playbook preview for non-subscribers
Give the AI a proactive greeting on login

The bones are good. The gap between here and something people can't stop
talking about is mostly depth and visualization make the data feel real,
make the empty states sell the vision, and let the AI actually coach
instead of waiting to be asked.
Keep building.
Shane
